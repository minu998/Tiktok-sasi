Use MP4 or MOV with H.264/HEVC encoding. For the best possible quality, export in 1080p or above, at a constant 60 FPS, with a high bitrate to preserve detail.
Our personal recommended bitrate is 20-50 Mbps depending on the resolution, to ensure the video plays smooth across all devices around the world. The goal is to get maximum quality with the lowest file size.
Once you edited your video and exported in 4K, then upload the final file to the TikTok Optimizer. RTXFury optimizes the MP4/MOV container so TikTok applies no compression to it, preserving your 4K resolution and frame rate, up to 4K 120 FPS on the Premium tier.
TikTok always compresses uploads, but you can keep near-lossless quality with RTX Fury TikTok Optimizer. Optimize your MP4/MOV with the TikTok Optimizer, enable Upload HD, and upload via TikTok web. DO NOT EDIT the video after optimizing. Upload it as is.
